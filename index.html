Letterhead to PDF
1 page
1

Letterhead

Pick a letterhead saved in this app, or upload your own. It becomes the A4 background of every page.

Later pages are plain white and keep the same side margins.

Writing area

Your text stays inside this area so it never touches the letterhead. It was detected automatically; adjust it if needed.

2

Heading

The title at the top of the letter, such as “Medical certificate”.

3

Letter content

Write or paste your letter. Long letters continue on new pages automatically.

To place the letter where you want it, drag the text on the preview. Drag the round handles to change its size.

Hindi and English both work. Noto fonts give the best Hindi. Pasted text is cleaned up to keep your letter tidy.

4

Signature, stamp and extras optional

Add a signature or stamp, a text box for a date or reference number, or erase something printed on the letterhead. Drag anything on the preview to place it.

5

Download

Check the preview, then save your letter as an A4 PDF.

Private by design. Your letterhead, signature and text stay in this browser and are never uploaded.
